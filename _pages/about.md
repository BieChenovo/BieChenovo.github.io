I am a research assistant in the [Physical AI and Robotic Systems Lab (PAIRS Lab)](https://pairslab.github.io/) at [The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)](https://www.hkust-gz.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Fangqiang Ding](https://fangqiangding.com/). I received my B.Eng. in Automation from [Tongji University](https://www.tongji.edu.cn/) in June 2026.

I am interested in **physical agents** that perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and act toward goals in the physical world. A question that guides my research is how an agent can learn from the consequences of its actions and adapt when conditions change. Robot navigation is one application of this broader interest.

## Research interests

**Spatial memory.** Spatial memory lets an agent connect observations over time and reason about places beyond its current view. The challenge is deciding what to remember, when to retrieve it, and how to update an earlier belief when the environment changes.

**Robot navigation.** Navigation brings perception, planning, and control together so a robot can reach a goal in an unfamiliar environment. With only a partial view, the robot must decide when it knows enough to follow a route and when further exploration is worth the time and motion.

**The sim-to-real gap.** Simulation lets researchers train and test agents across many conditions, but real sensors and hardware can respond differently to the same situation. I am interested in how to identify which differences matter for a decision and how to evaluate whether a policy still works when a physical robot executes it.
